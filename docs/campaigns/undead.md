# Undead campaign: Leave a Lamp

**High Executor Sereth Vale · Years Three–Five · Three missions**

All three chapters are available from Sereth’s campaign map as development playtests. After the Year Four clear, travel to Valley Assembly for the distinct finale briefing and deployment confirmation. See the [implementation index](implementation-index.md) for the current route and [visual inventory](../visual-audit-inventory.md) for what remains unapproved.

See [Sereth and the Last Testament](../leaders.md#undead--high-executor-sereth-vale), [the shared war timeline](../war-timeline.md), and [battlefield command](../combat.md#battlefield-command).

Sereth helped write the rule that the dead must consent to return. The court sends him for the Godbridle before another command can silence the voices at the cut. He has a public reason to obey and a private one: after two centuries, there may finally be something his sister left behind.

## 1. The Names at the Cut — Year Three (`sereth_voices_at_the_cut`)

**Briefing:** After a riverbank collapses beside the oldest graves, villagers hear voices address living people by name. Hold the village while its clerk takes accounts and recovers the exposed records.

- **Map and orders:** A cemetery stair climbs from the cut to the village; an open approach from the road leads to the record room. The stair is a narrow infantry hold. The road allows cavalry to charge; the walled lanes let Sereth and foot troops cross between the sites without giving the riders a straight run. Show the clerk and records as separate objectives, not as one escort.
- **Turn and win:** Speak to a villager, Iren, by the stair, then return to the record room to enter the account while the clerk prepares the recovered bundle. The villager heard her own name and recognized the voice. Record both as her account, not as confirmation that a dead person returned. Win when the account is entered and the bundle is secured inside the record room.
- **Pressure and failure:** March troops want the records out of reach before the village can blame their crown for the new channel. They can be stalled on the road or at the stair; loss of control near the record room pauses the clerk’s work and can be recovered. Mark an enemy attempt to seize the bundle and fail only when it leaves the marked retreat edge in enemy hands, or if Sereth falls. Sereth can recover a dropped bundle before the enemy extracts it.
- **Cost:** Sereth seals the records for cataloging instead of giving the court a list of names to recall. He leaves under commission to secure the Godbridle, before anyone has found his sister’s letter. Nothing in the sound, subtitle labels, or objective screen answers what the voices are.

## 2. An Audience, Not a Seizure — Year Four (`sereth_pass_writ`)

**Playtest:** After securing the records at the Cut, travel to **Keeper Boundary Post**, read the dispatch and confirm deployment. The Keeper’s reply grants an audience only. Withdraw to the west rally to record the clear and show **A Hearing Only**. The clear opens **Valley Assembly** and its finale briefing; arriving does not automatically deploy the mission.

**Briefing:** Mara has already handed the bridle to Sova. The court sends a writ requesting an audience before the vault is closed. A rival March patrol wants to stop the writ from reaching the valley Keeper and keep the next move for its own crown.

- **Map and orders:** The open road gives the patrol’s mounted reserve room to turn and charge. A sunken sheep track reaches the Keeper’s boundary post under cover, but only the foot force can take that approach. A lookout at a road bend can see a force leaving the track and signal the reserve. Spot the lookout from the higher bank or draw the mounted reserve onto the road with a visible infantry screen.
- **Turn and win:** Sereth carries the writ. Use formation orders to screen one approach while he reaches the boundary post and hands it to the neutral Keeper. The Keeper allows the court to present its claim at the valley assembly, not to enter the vault or use the bridle. Withdraw Sereth to the rally marker once the writ is accepted. The player chooses the approach and timing; no capture or pursuit of the bridle convoy can award victory.
- **Pressure and failure:** A signal horn and map marker show when the reserve commits. If alerted, it is a response to counter with a brace, covered retreat, or reroute, not immediate defeat. Fail if Sereth falls or if a hostile formation occupies the post long enough to drive the Keeper away before delivery. Give a visible warning and a chance to break the occupation.
- **Cost:** Keeping the court’s claim alive commits Sereth in its eyes. At the next briefing the court names its own speaker and orders Sereth to escort that official from the assembly to the vault. The writ never grants the speaker consent from the dead.

## 3. The Lamp in the Window — Year Five (`sereth_no_proxy`)

**Briefing:** At the valley assembly a clerk brings a letter found in the cataloged village records. Sereth’s sister asks him to leave a lamp in the window after she dies. She says nothing about returning. The court’s speaker still intends to ask Orrun on behalf of people who left no such permission.

- **Map and orders:** Use the [same Cairnspine road](dryads.md#3-a-road-we-can-share--year-five-neris_cairnspine_passage): the valley assembly and staging area, the narrow shelf toward the vault, and the side path that rejoins at the forecourt beside the safe terrace. Sereth starts with a household platoon and court-assigned troops. After he refuses the escort order, court-assigned troops leave his roster; some form the speaker’s escort. Telegraph that split before returning control. The household platoon stays with him.
- **Turn and win:** Interact at the assembly to refuse the escort in front of the witnesses. Screen the terrace approach against the rival company and intercept the speaker’s escort on either approach to the forecourt. Once the escort is stalled, reach the speaker as Sereth and use the challenge interaction before the marked custody line. This stops the bid to speak for the dead without making killing the speaker the objective. Win when the bid is halted and the terrace approach is clear for the witnesses to proceed. Their road journey belongs to Neris; custody and closure belong to Sova.
- **Pressure and failure:** Show which approach the speaker is taking. If the shelf is blocked, his party can use the side path; Sereth can reposition the household platoon and take the other route himself. Rival troops pressure the terrace while he is away. Fail if the speaker crosses the custody line before the challenge, if the defenders lose the witnesses’ safe position after a telegraphed enemy hold, or if Sereth falls. The custody line is the Undead mission’s last chance to stop the claim; crossing it never confirms a successful command to Orrun.
- **Cost:** The court strips Sereth of his commission for refusing its order. The bridle goes back into the vault without a new command. In the coda he leaves the lamp his sister asked for. Neither the letter nor the light suggests that she consented, refused, answered, or returned.

## Playtest targets

- **Tactical progression:** Split protection and recover a portable objective at the cut; use detection and a screen to create an opening for one courier at the boundary post; then switch sides of a shared route after losing court troops. Test each phase at base training, including the final defense with only the household platoon.
- **Training and ratings:** Charge & Brace improves the road hold; Scouting improves warning where there is line of sight; Formation Drill helps the household platoon reform between attacks. If a Curse attack is available to the roster, use its existing damage rules only. Curse does not reveal a voice’s source, translate a letter, or grant consent.
- **Continuity:** Verify the player can finish the final mission without carrying the bridle, opening the vault, or repeating Neris’s escort. Use the terrace as a position to defend, not an arrival counter. Sova’s and Neris’s wins remain independent. No optional objective may establish more about the voices than the witnesses actually heard.
