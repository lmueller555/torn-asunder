# Orc campaign: The First Draw

**War Marshal Raska Vennor · Years One–Four · Three missions**

Campaign design, not playable content. See [Raska’s mandate](../leaders.md#orcs--war-marshal-raska-vennor), [the shared war timeline](../war-timeline.md), and [combat and platoon training](../combat.md#platoon-training).

The Red Basin elected Raska to get its river back. The lower towns want the sea as well: the restored river will reach the Basin, but it will not give those towns back their pastures and shore trade. The old sea, in turn, would inundate the town that hosts the assembly. Raska’s victories let him fulfill his mandate, not settle every claim to the water.

## 1. One Person to Ask — Year One (`orc_mine_witness`)

**Briefing:** Capture someone who can account for the spring at the mine and the dry river in the Basin. The Godbridle is not the objective.

- **Map and orders:** One Orc infantry platoon pins the Human escort’s rearguard where the pass narrows. Send the second platoon up the east ridge ramp, across the crest using its cover, and down the west ramp toward the screened outlet approach. The Human archers can cover the road from the ridge, so do not leave the crew party on the exposed crest. The tunnel and Druid evacuation lane are not attack objectives.
- **Turn and win:** When the workers reach the tunnel outlet, one miner stops. She will tell her story at the assembly if the others can go. Return to Raska and interact to accept her terms. She follows Raska only after the remaining workers clear the outlet toward Druid safety. Escort her to the Orc rally point. The pass platoon can disengage as soon as the outlet is covered; killing the rearguard or stopping the bridle convoy is not a win condition.
- **Pressure and failure:** The rearguard sends a detachment to secure the outlet approach if the pass screen lets it through; the player can recover by moving the outlet platoon into its path or intercepting it as Raska. Fail if Raska or the witness falls, or if the detachment holds the marked outlet approach until the last escape window closes. Telegraph that window with the workers approaching the outlet and a marked enemy advance, not a hidden timer.
- **Cost:** Raska gives up the pursuit even when the bridle is still in sight. In the debrief, the miner’s account and Sova’s survey place the spring and the loss of the river on the same night. Mara retains the bridle, and the rest of the crew escapes with Sova on the [same battlefield](../combat.md#year-one-battle-at-the-mine).

## 2. The Second Bucket — Year Three (`orc_well_road`)

**Briefing:** Reports from Year Two say Mara moved the water again. The new cut has left the Red Basin’s well road carrying the traffic its river used to serve. A rival March company comes for the well carts after its own supply fails.

- **Map and orders:** Get an upper-well cart and a lower-well cart through the Basin exit. The open salt road is shorter but gives Human cavalry a charge lane. The winding wash blocks sight and denies a straight run-up, but its single ramp is a choke point. Brace infantry on the road or post them at the ramp, and put archers on the wash shoulder for a clear shot at anything that tries to turn the screen.
- **Turn and win:** Raska opens the lower well’s storehouse to households that have no well right, including March refugees. The second cart only starts once those households are aboard. The first can leave immediately. Win when both carts reach the exit. The player chooses route and spacing, not who is entitled to a seat.
- **Pressure and failure:** Watch the mounted platoon turn and form up on the salt road before committing a cart. If the brace breaks, fall back to the crooked wash, reform the infantry, and cover the ramp. Fail if either required cart is destroyed or Raska falls.
- **Cost:** The lower-town captain lends her company for the escort, then counts the last barrels onto the cart herself. She asks Raska what his restored river will give towns beyond its banks. He can promise a river, not a sea. The Spanwater briefing pays off that exchange: the lower companies will help reach the lock, but they expect the sea gate opened.

## 3. River, Not Sea — Year Four (`orc_spanwater_release`)

**Briefing:** The breach at Spanwater Lock gives the Basin a way to take some of the diverted water back without the Godbridle. The lock has two distinct controls. The **spillway** feeds the old Basin riverbed; the **sea gate** admits water to the old seabed and would inundate the assembly town. Label and show both routes before deployment.

- **Map and orders:** From the upstream approach, use the raised service causeway to reach the lock controls. The exposed apron lets March defenders mass for counterattacks; the lock stairs provide cover for an infantry approach but are too tight to ride through. The low crossing gives a shortcut across the riverbed before release and becomes impassable afterward. The town’s feeder branch with its maintenance path and the low landing with its boat exit are separate lanes, not Orc capture points.
- **Turn and win:** Clear the spillway wheel and use Raska’s interact to start the release. The lower-town captain asks for the sea gate too. Raska refuses; her companies leave by the approach, still friendly but no longer selectable, before the last counterattack. Hold the spillway clear with the remaining platoons until the river-stage indicator is reached and then bring Raska to the high-bank marker. The sea gate never becomes a player objective.
- **Pressure and failure:** Warn the player before the release cuts the low crossing and identify the high exit in both camera views. A March defender reaching the wheel pauses release until the player clears the control zone; warn when an uninterrupted enemy hold is about to shut it. Fail if the defenders shut the spillway before river stage or if Raska falls. The final counterattack must be beatable with the regulars alone and no purchased training.
- **Cost and handoff:** The Basin gets its river, not the sea; the lower towns recall their companies and the Marches lose some of their diverted water. Show the lower-town standards leaving instead of treating their departure as defeat in battle. On the shared map Raska sets the flow, Neris keeps the town feeder open, and Ilyra evacuates stranded households by boat. Neither other task is an Orc victory requirement. A short coda can show Raska witnessing Mara hand the Godbridle to Sova; there is no player pursuit or Orc custody of the artifact.

## Playtest targets

- **Command and hero progression:** Start with two infantry platoons at the mine and teach concurrent orders while Raska reaches the outlet. Add an archer platoon for the well road and teach the value of bracing and reforming against cavalry. At Spanwater, test reading the release, changing routes, and replacing a withdrawn company’s screen without leaving the wheel exposed.
- **Training payoffs:** Use the existing Scouting, Charge & Brace, and Formation Drill rules to reward early detection, sustained holds, and recovery after a charge. If campaign XP hooks are available, reward objective completion rather than enemy kills; keep base-training clears viable. Exact rewards, roster counts, and hold durations are tuning values, not new combat rules.
- **Continuity checks:** At the mine all three canonical results must coexist. At Spanwater verify that the river-stage clear leaves the sea gate shut, the lower companies gone, and the feeder and boat lanes available for their own campaigns. Neither mission requires simulating a different campaign’s win before awarding its own.

Design reference only: [*Kingdom Under Fire: Heroes* manual, pp. 12–25](https://manuals.plus/m/77bff80c4c8a7cc0c78419ed521a33be6d31fa0b7551d7ecba9d3abc949bc5a2.pdf) for troop orders, hero combat and control points. The campaign’s characters, events, and objectives are original.

## Spanwater objective rules

The standalone `raska_spanwater_release.gd` model accepts observations from a future mission scene. Orc control unlocks a **new** Raska interaction at `SpillwayWheel`, which orders the lower-town withdrawal once and starts the release. Only fresh progress reported while the wheel is secured counts toward river stage. Contest or lost control pauses it; a battlefield-confirmed shutdown before river stage fails it. Once river stage is reached, Raska must reach `HighBankExit`. The model has no countdown or sea-gate action. The model does not provide visuals or place units. Verify the phase rules with `godot --headless --script tests/raska_spanwater_release.gd`.

Run `godot game/battlefield/raska_spanwater.tscn` for the standalone campaign-scale graybox. Follow the causeway to the wheel, order a formation into the inner ring to push back the March guard, then return to Raska and press **Cross / Space** at the wheel. The lower-town companies depart as friendlies and stop taking orders. The regulars can screen the approaching guard or push it back to resume release. A simple moving ribbon marks the river’s advance; at river stage the authored low-crossing zone closes to movement and the high-bank exit becomes active. This is not fluid simulation, full troop combat, or campaign progression. Run `godot --headless --script tests/raska_spanwater.gd` to verify the playable encounter.

## Well Road objective rules

The standalone `raska_well_road.gd` model starts the upper cart ready and waits for the battlefield to confirm that households have finished boarding the lower cart. A premature lower-cart order does not queue. After the ready state, each cart can take an admitted route and can switch between salt road and wash if the battlefield permits it. Terrain, cavalry pressure, and optional formation positions belong to the scene, not the win check. Only two intact arrivals clear the escort; actual cart loss or Raska’s incapacitation fails it. There is no deadline or partial clear. Run `godot --headless --script tests/raska_well_road.gd` to verify the objective rules.

Run `godot game/battlefield/raska_well_road_playtest.tscn` for the standalone [Red Basin](../red-basin-well-road-map.md) graybox. Select either cart with **L1 / C**, order **Salt road** with **R1 / G**, or **Wash** with **Triangle / T**. Approach the lower storehouse as Raska and press **Cross / Space** to start boarding; wait for the households to reach the cart, then issue a fresh route order. A cart entering the salt road brings the mounted reserve out on a visible approach. You can hold a formation near its blocking position to screen it or reroute the wagon back through the fork to the wash. Neither route nor formation position is required for victory: both carts must reach the Basin exit. This prototype shows interdiction, not cavalry charges or combat damage. In Raska’s campaign, the Mine clear opens the route to Basin Well Road. The Well Road debrief follows the clear with both carts, and that clear opens travel to Spanwater Lock, which has no campaign deployment yet. Launching this scene directly does not touch the campaign save. Run `godot --headless --script tests/raska_well_road_playtest.gd` to verify the scene.
