# Druid campaign: Name Who Pays

First Listener Sova Cairn begins by keeping people out of the armies' hands. He ends by agreeing that his own people should not get to hide what they did with the Godbridle. His guards can defend sacred ground; the Keepers decide when their valleys will send guards beyond the Cairnspine. See [Sova's authority](../leaders.md#druids--first-listener-sova-cairn) and [the war timeline](../war-timeline.md#six-campaign-windows).

## 1. The Workers' Way Out

**Briefing.** Sova's survey puts the new spring on the same night as the river's disappearance. What it cannot tell the Basin is what the miners said to Orrun. Mara's escort is moving on the pass; fighting it would leave the workers where both armies can reach them. With the volunteers who came to help his survey, open the old tunnels and get the workers out first.

**Victory.** Send both marked work groups through the tunnel outlet to Druid safety. The miner who volunteers to speak with Raska is with the work groups through the tunnels. At the outlet she waits under a truce; she remains safe there for the Druid objective once the others are clear, and can then walk to the Orc rally point on the terms established in [Raska's mine mission](orcs.md). The Orc rally point is not a Druid destination. **Defeat:** Sova falls, either work group is lost before safety, or the Human rear detachment holds the outlet approach until the last escape window closes. Show the same approaching detachment and announced final window as in Raska's view; no hidden clock.

**Teach: release civilians in stages through a choke point.** Fellow Druids hold the tunnel mouth while Sova checks the outlet. Stage each group in tunnel cover and release it with an order; show the progress of both groups in the command view. The first is near the mouth and ready immediately; the second waits at a bend where evacuees are screened from the ridge. Send one while the outlet is clear or briefly keep both in cover and move the screen forward first. If the rearguard contests the marked approach, recover it before the final window. Do not send Druid guards to the Orc rally or take the Godbridle road exit.

**Result and transition.** Mara's carrier reaches the road exit; Sova's workers get through the tunnels, and the miner goes with Raska voluntarily after seeing the others safe. A copy of her testimony travels with Sova's survey. Hearing what one unrecorded request for water did, Sova decides he must read the older, deliberate command in public, including the names his predecessors withheld.

## 2. Let Them Walk Out

**Briefing.** After the reports from Moonwash, Sova brings the sealed record to the valley assembly. He intends to read the command to raise the Cairnspine, who gave it, and what it cost the coast and Basin. Several Keepers will leave rather than hear it; armed spoilers want to stop the reading and take the record. Walking out is not an attack, and a Keeper who leaves must not become a target.

**Victory.** Start the hearing from either marked lectern. Complete all three passages with the record and at least one public scribe present. The chosen lectern fixes the neutral hearing ring and its public exit for the attempt; show both before committing. **Defeat:** Sova falls, an armed spoiler takes the record from a contested lectern after the displayed seizure window, or no scribe remains. A charge across the ring, public exit blocked by troops, or Sova leaving the lectern interrupts the current passage; cleared passages stay read. A clear ring and open exit let the player resume. Keepers leaving do not start a countdown or cancel the hearing.

**Teach: hold a perimeter without taking the floor.** The open dais has two approaches visible from the command view; the recessed lectern gives cover and a narrower attack approach, but puts the public exit close to that approach. At either setting, use troops to hold outside the ring and intercept pressure before it reaches the hearing. Threats alternate between an attempt on the record and one near the public exit: committing both formations to the first can close the exit or leave the second uncovered. Sova reads in short, interruptible sections with allied orders still running. Do not conflate holding the record with occupying every path to it.

**Result and transition.** Sova finishes the reading. Several Keepers leave anyway. A scribe makes a copy available for Ilyra and other parties; it is no longer possible for a single Keeper to put the event back under seal. In the debrief a guard asks whether to stop the departing Keepers. Sova lets them pass: consent to fight for the vault in Year Five cannot come from people held at spearpoint.

**Year Four interlude.** After the breach at Spanwater, Mara hands the Godbridle to Sova in sight of Orc and Elven witnesses. Show the seal opened only to verify what changes hands, not a new command or a transfer of ownership by treaty. Cut to the Year Five assembly at the foot of the pass. Enough Keepers pledge guards for the vault road after Sova reads the full account into the custody terms; no First Listener can seal it again. Rival crowns and the Still Kingdom have not accepted those terms and approach the pass.

## 3. No New Command

**Briefing.** The vault is ahead of the assembly, beyond a narrow shelf. A side path rejoins the shelf route at the vault forecourt. The witness terrace is adjacent but has its own marked entry. Sova's task is to return the Godbridle to the vault; neither he nor his guards may speak a command into it. Neris is bringing civilians and witnesses up the road. Sereth's quarrel with his court is not Sova's objective.

**Victory.** Bring Sova carrying the bridle from the valley assembly to the vault forecourt, establish a clear approach, and complete the return interaction at the vault. A hostile unit inside the marked closure zone pauses the action until cleared; the carrier simply reaching the forecourt is not a clear. **Defeat:** Sova falls before closure. For this mission Sova is the sole carrier; contested closure and retreat remain recoverable while he lives. The witness terrace stays accessible, but no particular civilian route, arrival count, or Undead outcome gates this victory.

**Teach: choose the carrier route and defend its far end.** The shelf is short and shielded on one side but leaves little room to reform. One Keeper platoon can hold an advance there while Sova takes the side path, or Sova can take the shelf while a flanking platoon reaches the forecourt by the side path. Show a hostile reserve turning toward the forecourt before it arrives. The clear requires protecting the carrier and denying that reserve entry during closure, not pursuing fleeing claimants or filling the map with friendly troops. Neither path may permanently close before the player's choice.

**Result and ending.** Sova returns the bridle without speaking a command. Orc, Elven and Human witnesses attest to what happened from the separate terrace. The Godbridle is out of the war; Orrun is still carrying out Mara's order. End on one remaining duty: Sova directs that the vault's record stay where any valley can read it, even when the people reading it have reason to blame him.

## Shared geography and acceptance

- **Mine:** One map with camp, pass, ridge, tunnels, and outlet. The Human bridle exit and Orc rally remain distinct from Druid work-group safety. A canonical clear leaves Mara holding the artifact, gives Raska a consenting witness, and gets the other workers out. No other campaign's win flag is a prerequisite.
- **Valley:** Reuse the valley ground from the hearing as the [Cairnspine pass](../war-timeline.md#where-perspectives-can-overlap) staging and safe assembly in the finale. Across Druid, Dryad, and Undead views keep the foot of the pass, narrow shelf, rejoining side path, forecourt, and separate witness terrace in the same places. Change spawns and objective ownership, not the geography. Script shared background results independently when needed.
- **Tests:** Try the mine with both release orders and under outlet pressure. Finish the hearing at both lecterns, with a deliberate interruption and with Keepers walking out; verify that a public exit blocked by friendly troops does not complete a passage. Reach the vault from each route; contest and resume closure; confirm Druid completion does not depend on another faction's mission status. Failed or replayed attempts must not advance the history or persist a revised ending.
