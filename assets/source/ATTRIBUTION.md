# Human Infantry asset sources

The Human Infantry uses the MakeHuman base mesh, adult male and facial shape targets, default skin weights, and brown eye asset under CC0 1.0. The body was adapted to the Infantry skeleton and fitted with original garments, helmet, sword, shield, and material maps. The eye texture was recolored for this asset.
